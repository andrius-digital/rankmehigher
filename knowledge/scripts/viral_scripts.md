# 5 Viral Social Media Scripts for CDL Agency

**Filming Context:** To be filmed at Bobby Loads' professional studio. The tone should be direct, confident, and value-driven, in the style of Alex Hormozi and Bobby Loads.

---

### Script 1: The Market Is Turning

**(Hook)**
The freight recession is over.

For the first time since 2023, the market is in your favor.

Spot rates are climbing.

Flatbed is up 13% from last year.

Reefer load-to-truck ratios hit 26 to 1.

That’s pandemic-level demand.

Carriers are fighting for trucks.

Don’t get left behind.

This is the time to make your move.

---

### Script 2: CPM is a Scam

**(Hook)**
Stop letting them pay you by the mile.

It’s a scam.

When freight rates go up, your CPM stays the same.

The carrier keeps the extra cash.

You don’t see a dime of it.

In a rising market, you want percentage of gross.

That way, when the carrier makes more money...

...you make more money.

Know the difference.

Demand your share.

---

### Script 3: Your Next Recruiter is Free

**(Hook)**
Your next recruiter is free.

Here’s how you know if a carrier is serious about hiring you.

Ask them if they paid for your application.

Most carriers use recruiters who get paid to send them stacks of resumes.

They have no skin in the game.

But some carriers use pay-per-driver agencies.

They only pay AFTER you’re hired and in the truck.

That means they’re invested in you from day one.

Work with people who have skin in the game.

---

### Script 4: The Reefer Squeeze

**(Hook)**
This is the hottest freight in America right now.

Reefer.

Rates are pushing $3.00 a mile.

Why?

Winter storms forced regular freight into temperature-controlled trucks.

Then Valentine’s Day hit.

Over 4,000 loads of flowers came out of Miami in two weeks.

The load-to-truck ratio exploded.

If you’re pulling a reefer, you should be printing money.

If you’re not, you’re leaving cash on the table.

---

### Script 5: Don’t Go Broke in a Good Market

**(Hook)**
A lot of owner-operators are about to go broke.

Sounds crazy, right?

The market is finally turning.

But after a long recession, guys get desperate.

They see rates go up and they run out and buy a brand new rig.

They take on massive debt.

One bad breakdown and they’re done.

Don’t be that guy.

Stack cash.

Build your maintenance fund.

Fix your current truck.

Profit now, buy new toys later.
