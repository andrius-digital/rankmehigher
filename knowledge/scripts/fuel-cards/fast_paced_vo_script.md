# Fast-Paced Voiceover Script: The Fuel Card Sales Playbook

**Style:** Alex Hormozi tone — casual, direct, confident. Like breaking down the game plan to a business partner over coffee.
**Pacing:** Fast, no dead air, compact like 1.2x speed. Every sentence delivers value.
**Background:** Lofi beat, low volume.
**Visual:** Loom-style walkthrough. Screen share with strategy doc, whiteboard, or slides behind the voiceover.
**Target Length:** ~2.5 minutes.

---

(0:00) Alright, here is the exact playbook for how we are going to sell fuel cards to small fleets.

(0:04) Three to twenty trucks, that's our target.

(0:06) First, let me tell you what doesn't work so we can stop wasting money on it.

(0:10) Facebook ads — dead end.

(0:12) Fleet owners are not scrolling Instagram looking for a new financial partner.

(0:15) VSL funnels — same problem.

(0:17) You cannot sell a complex, case-by-case pricing product through a one-way video.

(0:21) These are operational decisions, not impulse buys.

(0:24) Now here is what actually works.

(0:26) Play number one — and this is the big one — the FMCSA database.

(0:30) Every time a new trucking authority goes active, it hits a public government database.

(0:34) That fleet owner now needs insurance, factoring, and fuel cards immediately.

(0:38) We set up an AI scraper that pulls new DOT numbers the second they appear.

(0:42) We filter for three to twenty trucks.

(0:44) We fire off a personalized email automatically.

(0:47) Then a rep calls within thirty minutes.

(0:49) This is not cold calling — this is showing up with the exact solution they need on the exact day they need it.

(0:54) The first company to reach them wins, and that is going to be us.

(0:58) Play number two — channel partners.

(1:00) We are not going to sell this alone.

(1:02) We are going to recruit the people these fleet owners already trust.

(1:06) Independent dispatchers, freight brokers, diesel mechanics, even CDL schools.

(1:10) We build a referral program that pays real money — two fifty to five hundred bucks per activated fleet.

(1:15) If a dispatcher can save their carrier money on fuel, that dispatcher becomes a hero.

(1:19) We give mechanics referral cards that highlight our Michelin tire discounts and roadside assistance.

(1:24) We are borrowing trust from people who already have it.

(1:27) Play number three — capture the active buyers.

(1:30) When a fleet owner goes to Google and types "best fuel card for five trucks," we need to own that search result.

(1:36) Targeted Google Ads, long-tail keywords, specific landing page.

(1:40) The landing page doesn't say "sign up."

(1:42) It says "request a custom fleet analysis."

(1:45) We make it about their specific routes, their specific volume, their specific business.

(1:49) Play number four — YouTube content.

(1:52) We build a channel that teaches small fleet owners how to run their business better.

(1:56) Cost per mile breakdowns, cash flow management, what to do when a truck breaks down at two AM.

(2:00) We naturally weave in the fuel card benefits without being salesy.

(2:04) This builds owned trust over time, and it compounds.

(2:07) Now here is the timeline.

(2:09) Days one through fourteen — set up the FMCSA scraper, write the scripts, build the landing page, finalize the referral program.

(2:15) Days fifteen through forty-five — launch outbound, launch Google Ads, start recruiting partners.

(2:20) Days forty-six through ninety — launch YouTube, optimize based on data, attend a trucking event.

(2:25) The competition is out there selling a feeling.

(2:28) We are selling better margins, included roadside, tire discounts, and a real operational partnership.

(2:33) That is the game plan.

(2:35) Let's go execute.
